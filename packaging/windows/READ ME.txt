Synaxis for Windows
===================

Your movies and shows, like a streaming service, played in VLC.

Before you start
  Install VLC: https://www.videolan.org/vlc/
  (Synaxis finds it in its usual place; otherwise set the VLC program in Settings.)

Start
  Double-click synaxis.exe. The first time, Synaxis looks for videos larger
  than 200 MB in your user folder; choose other folders under Settings
  (the profile menu, top right).

  If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unrecognised app, choose
  "More info", then "Run anyway".

Quit
  Close the window, or "Quit Synaxis" in the profile menu.

Your settings and history
  Settings:                        %APPDATA%\Synaxis\config.json
  Library, history and thumbnails: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Synaxis
  Deleting the Synaxis folder removes the program; these stay.

Watch Together
  Watch with up to 8 people in other places, each from their own copy of the
  video. See "Watch Together" in the top bar.
